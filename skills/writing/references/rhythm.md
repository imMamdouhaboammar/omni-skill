# Rhythm and Scannability in Technical Prose

Designing scannable structure to avoid reader fatigue.

## Principles
1. **Scannable Headers**: Use action-oriented, informative headers that summarize key concepts rather than generic labels like "Introduction" or "Overview".
2. **Lists Over Dense Paragraphs**: When presenting three or more parallel conditions, steps, or alternatives, format them as bullet points or numbered lists.
3. **Varying Cadence**: Alternate between short, authoritative assertion sentences and concise technical payloads.
4. **Zero Conversational Noise**: Eliminate throat-clearing preambles ("It is important to remember that...", "In this section, we will..."). Start immediately with the payload.
